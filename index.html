How to use

  1. Spray: apply the product undiluted, evenly over the sink surface.
  2. Spread & wait: spread with a soft cloth, sponge or brush and leave for 2–5 minutes to break down limescale and stains.
  3. Scrub & rinse: scrub lightly in circular motions, then rinse thoroughly with warm, clean water.
  4. Repeat if needed: for stubborn limescale or heavy staining, repeat or leave on for longer before scrubbing.

Before you start: Wear gloves and eye protection. Do not breathe spray, and use in a well-ventilated area (open a window or turn on the extractor fan). Test on a small, hidden area first if unsure.

Safety information

Warning H315: Causes skin irritation. H319: Causes serious eye irritation.

Contains: fatty alcohol ethoxylate, sulphamic acid.

Full safety information: precautions, first aid, storage and disposal

Before use

  • P102: Keep out of reach of children.
  • P103: Read label before use.
  • P260: Do not breathe spray.
  • P280: Wear protective gloves/protective clothing/eye protection/face protection.
  • Ensure there is sufficient ventilation of the area.
  • Do not mix with bleach or other cleaners. Not suitable for unsealed natural stone, marble, terrazzo, enamel or plated metals.

First aid

  • IF IN EYES: Rinse cautiously with water for several minutes. Remove contact lenses, if present and easy to do. Continue rinsing. (P305+P351+P338) If eye irritation persists: Get medical advice/attention. (P337+P313)
  • IF ON SKIN (or hair): Take off immediately all contaminated clothing. Rinse skin with water. (P303+P361+P353)
  • IF SWALLOWED: Rinse mouth. Do NOT induce vomiting. (P301+P330+P331)
  • IF INHALED: Remove person to fresh air and keep comfortable for breathing. (P304+P340)

Storage and disposal

  • Store in a cool, well ventilated area. Keep container tightly closed. Conditions to avoid: heat, freezing.
  • Empty bottle: Clean with water. Recycle container where permitted.

Frequently asked questions

Which sinks can I use Astraclean on?

Composite, granite and quartz sinks, including black, white and coloured sinks. Don't use it on unsealed natural stone or acid-sensitive surfaces such as marble, terrazzo, enamel or plated metals. If unsure, test a small hidden area first.

Will it scratch my sink?

Astraclean is made to clean without harsh abrasives. Apply it with a soft cloth, sponge or brush and scrub lightly.

How long do I leave it on?

2–5 minutes, then scrub lightly and rinse thoroughly with clean water. For heavy limescale or staining, repeat or leave it on longer.

Does it remove limescale and water marks?

Yes. It tackles hard-water deposits and mineral build-up and leaves a clean, even finish, so it's ideal in hard-water areas.

Can I mix it with bleach or other cleaners?

No. Never mix Astraclean with bleach or any other cleaner.

3. Website statistics

Google Analytics 4 (only if you accept)

If you accept analytics cookies, we use Google Analytics 4 (measurement ID G-CVNBPXH52L), provided by Google Ireland Limited and Google LLC, to produce statistics about how our website is used. Google Analytics sets cookies (such as _ga and _ga_CVNBPXH52L) and collects the usage data described above. Google Analytics 4 doesn't log or store full IP addresses.

  • Google Analytics is only loaded after you click "Accept" in our cookie banner. If you click "Reject", or make no choice, it isn't loaded at all.
  • Analytics data is kept for the period set in our Google Analytics settings, which is never more than 14 months, and is then deleted automatically.
  • Google may process this data in the United States. See section 6.
  • More information: how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also install Google's opt-out browser add-on.
  • If you accept, Google Analytics also records clicks on our Buy direct, eBay and Amazon buttons (as an event called store_click) as part of your visit.

Counting clicks on our buy buttons (no cookies)

When you click a Buy direct, eBay or Amazon button, your browser sends a short anonymous message to Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a free counting service run by Jason Cameron, which may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. It adds one to a few counters: one for the shop you chose, one for the page and part of the page the button was on, and ones for the month, day and hour (UK time). We use these totals to see which ways of buying our customers use, and to compare clicks with orders by date and time.

  • No cookies are used, and nothing is stored on or read from your device, so this happens whether or not you accept analytics cookies.
  • We only send the counter names. There are no identifiers and no details about you or your order, and we've told your browser not to send the address of the page you were on. We can't tell who clicked.
  • As with any website your browser connects to, the counting service also receives the standard technical details of the connection, such as your IP address and browser type. They aren't part of the counts, and we don't receive them.
  • If the counting service isn't available, the buttons work exactly the same.

5. Who we share your information with

We only share your information where we need to, with:

  • Our website host: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), which serves this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Our email provider: ImprovMX, which receives emails sent to sales@astracleanuk.com and forwards them to our mailbox.
  • Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev): our click-counting service, which receives only anonymous click counts (and, to make the connection, your IP address), as described in section 3.
  • Payment providers: if you buy direct, the payment provider shown at checkout (such as Stripe or PayPal). They act as independent controllers for payment and fraud-prevention data under their own privacy policies.
  • Delivery companies: such as Royal Mail or Evri, who receive your name, delivery address and sometimes your email or phone number so they can deliver your order and send tracking updates.
  • Google: for Google Analytics (only if you accept) and YouTube (only if you play the video), as described above.
  • Professional advisers and HMRC, where needed for tax and accounts.
  • Anyone we're legally required to share it with, for example the police or a court.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers (for example GitHub, Google, Stripe and PayPal) may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they protect it with recognised safeguards, such as the UK–US "data bridge" (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

The Abacus click counter may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. It only receives the anonymous counts described in section 3 (and, to make the connection, your IP address).

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you (a "subject access request");
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
  • restrict or object to our use of your information, including any use based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, meaning you get a copy of information you gave us in a reusable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example to analytics cookies) without affecting what we did before.

To use any of these rights, email sales@astracleanuk.com. There's no charge, and we'll reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first at sales@astracleanuk.com and we'll do our best to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, helpline 0303 123 1113.

10. Keeping your information safe

We use reputable, secure providers. The website runs over HTTPS (encrypted), payments are handled entirely by our payment provider, and access to our email and order records is password-protected and limited to us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.

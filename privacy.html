This website, www.astracleanuk.com, is run by Adam Marshall, trading as Astraclean UK ("we", "us", "our"). We are the controller of the personal information described in this policy, which means we decide how and why it's used.

If you have any question about this policy or your information, email us at sales@astracleanuk.com.

5. Who we share your information with

We only share your information where we need to, with:

  • Our website host: GitHub, Inc. (GitHub Pages), which serves this website and may log technical data such as IP addresses for security.
  • Our email provider: GoDaddy, which hosts the sales@astracleanuk.com mailbox.
  • Payment providers: if you buy direct, the payment provider shown at checkout (such as Stripe or PayPal). They act as independent controllers for payment and fraud-prevention data under their own privacy policies.
  • Delivery companies: such as Royal Mail or Evri, who receive your name, delivery address and sometimes your email or phone number so they can deliver your order and send tracking updates.
  • Google: for Google Analytics (only if you accept) and YouTube (only if you play the video), as described above.
  • Professional advisers and HMRC, where needed for tax and accounts.
  • Anyone we're legally required to share it with, for example the police or a court.

6. International transfers

Some of these providers (for example GitHub, Google, Stripe and PayPal) may process data outside the UK, including in the United States. Where they do, they protect it with recognised safeguards, such as the UK–US "data bridge" (the UK Extension to the EU–US Data Privacy Framework), the UK International Data Transfer Agreement, or the UK Addendum to the EU Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Your rights

Under UK data protection law you have the right to:

  • access the personal information we hold about you (a "subject access request");
  • have inaccurate information corrected;
  • have your information deleted where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
  • restrict or object to our use of your information, including any use based on legitimate interests;
  • data portability, meaning you get a copy of information you gave us in a reusable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time (for example to analytics cookies) without affecting what we did before.

To use any of these rights, email sales@astracleanuk.com. There's no charge, and we'll reply within one month.

9. Complaints

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, please contact us first at sales@astracleanuk.com and we'll do our best to put it right. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO): ico.org.uk, helpline 0303 123 1113.

10. Keeping your information safe

We use reputable, secure providers. The website runs over HTTPS (encrypted), payments are handled entirely by our payment provider, and access to our email and order records is password-protected and limited to us.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be on this page, with the date at the top.
